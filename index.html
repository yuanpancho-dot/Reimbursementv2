1. When you create an expense

Save a copy of the receipt into the Reimbursement folder on your laptop, and name the file using this format:

Category - Date - Account - Amount

Examples

Grab - Sep 2 2026 - Aboitiz Power - 205.00
Meals - Sep 2 2026 - Meltwater Summit - 306.00

2. Once a reimbursement is approved

Move all its receipts, still in the same Reimbursement folder, into an Archived subfolder. Mark them as approved so you know at a glance it's already been paid — you're just keeping a copy on file.

Archived - Amount Approved

This means: it's been paid, but keep the copy for your records.
